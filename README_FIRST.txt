UPLOAD TO HOSTING
1. Upload all files to public_html (or your chosen web folder).
2. Ensure PHP 8+ is selected.
3. Ensure PDO SQLite is enabled.
4. Ensure these folders are writable:
   /data
   /uploads
5. Visit /admin.php.
6. Upload your Expedia TAAP and TBO certificates.
7. Add your daily flight offers.
8. Replace stock/remote images later with your own agency photography if preferred.

DATABASE
/data/mustafa_travels.sqlite is created automatically on first visit.

OFFERS
Every offer uploaded in Admin can include:
title, subtitle, route, airline, price, currency, travel dates, baggage, badge and image.
